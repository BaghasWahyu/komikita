KomiKita is an open-source manga, comic, and webtoon reader for Android 8.0 and later.

Your comics, always close. Organize your collection, read at your own pace, and keep your reading progress with you.

Features:
- Customize page and webtoon reading modes, reading direction, themes, and controls.
- Organize your library with categories, search, and filters.
- Download chapters for offline reading.
- Hide categories and their comics, or hide extensions and their sources from Browse without uninstalling them.
- Optionally display extension icon badges in your library.
- Track reading progress and schedule library updates.
- Back up and restore your library and settings.
- Use the reader battery saver setting to prefer display refresh rates up to 60 Hz.

Telemetry and the built-in app updater are disabled by default. Project documentation is available in English and Bahasa Indonesia.

KomiKita is based on Mihon and Tachiyomi, with credit to their teams and contributors. It does not host content, provide extensions, or have any affiliation with content providers.
